About Me

I’m a small indie editor working inside my own cozy bubble. My thing is gaming videos that don’t take themselves too seriously – memes (visual and audible), funny cuts, goofy music, and sound effects that catch you off guard. But I also know when to pull back and let the gameplay speak for itself, so there’s always a balance between chaos and actual polish.

I live in Premiere Pro, After Effects, and Photoshop. I’m also an amateur thumbnail creator – still leveling up, but getting better every day.
