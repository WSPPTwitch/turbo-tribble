About Me

I’m a small video editor working inside my bubble. My thing is gaming videos that don’t take themselves too seriously. I add memes, funny cuts, and sound effects that catch people off guard. But I also know when to let the gameplay speak for itself. I am from Poland but do not worry, I can speak and transcribe English pretty fluently.

I work in Premiere Pro, After Effects, and Photoshop. I’m also an amateur thumbnail creator (but I'm still learning).
